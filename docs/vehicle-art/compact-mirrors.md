# 全车型小后视镜 — 2026-10-25

> Release cleanup (2026-10-26): historical screenshots and previews were archived. Old image paths below record provenance only; see [cleanup record](../ASSET_CLEANUP.md).

> 图片清理说明（2026-10-25）：本文保留历史方案与提示词；已淘汰的车辆图片和旧对比图已删除，文内旧路径仅作历史记录。当前资源见[车辆素材索引](README.md)。

用户要求所有后视镜缩小，外沿不得超过轮胎最外侧。使用内置 ImageGen，分别编辑小轿车五款、小货车四款、大卡车四款。镜壳缩为贴近驾驶室的小短块，保留小高光；不加宽车身或轮胎来满足限制。

运行素材保存在 `src/assets/vehicles/`：`mirror-car.png`及blue/sage/cream/lavender四色，`mirror-van.png`及blue/sage/lavender三色，`mirror-truck.png`及coral/sage/ochre三色。分别为768、512、768像素宽，透明裁切后等比缩小，保留生成的alpha，不做程序改色或局部拉伸。原素材、源图、最终路径、裁切、主体定位及边界检查见 `compact-mirrors.json`。

源图和最终运行PNG都按alpha>64检测：在轮胎参考列取上下最外边缘，前舱镜子所在的整个列区间都不能越过这两条边界。13款全部通过。轮胎世界宽度相对原版变化在约-2.2%到+0.3%，没有通过加宽轮胎规避限制；车体尺寸变化小于5%。维持单一等比绘制，物理尺寸、驾驶、关卡和固定配色分配不变。

实际手机视口截图为 `mirrors-car-in-game.png`、`mirrors-van-in-game.png`、`mirrors-truck-in-game.png`，均390×844；三块场地原尺寸裁切合并为 `compact-mirrors-in-game.png`。前后素材对比 `compact-mirrors-comparison.png` 使用同一世界倍率，新版绿线代表轮胎外沿。资源、现有玩法/布局和格式检查通过，浏览器错误日志为空。

## 完整提示词

### mirror-car

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: exact supplied production top-down mobile parking-game orange compact car sprite. Change ONLY the TWO WING MIRRORS beside the front cab. They are currently too large and protrude too far sideways. Replace them with tiny compact rounded mirror stubs, around HALF their current length and HALF width, tucked tightly against the cab edge. CRITICAL geometrical constraint: their OUTERMOST silhouette including black outline must lie INSIDE the two straight lines defined by the outer edges of the black TIRES on each side. No part of either mirror may project farther up/down than the outermost tire tabs. Leave a small inward clearance, aboutone quarter of the tire projection, so the tires define the vehicle's total transverse width. No long stalks, no large diagonal ears; tiny body-colored housing with one small glass highlight, symmetric pair. Keep mirrors readable but understated. Preserve EXACT body/cargo/cab shapes, square rear where present, whole chassis length/width proportions, wheel positions and widths, all paint colors, windows, reflection, wiper, lights, straps/folds and black contour style. DO NOT widen tires or body to satisfy the constraint, do not remove mirrors completely, do not change anything beyond mirrors and their attachments. Strict orthographic overhead, frontRIGHT, bilateral symmetry, no perspective. Whole vehicle on genuine transparent alpha, preserve reference framing/aspect; no text/logo/badge/ground shadow/glow/halo. Tire width is the hard maximum for mirror span.
```
